# Community conduct

Be respectful and keep discussion focused on the software. Constructive disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, spam and disclosure of private information are not.

Never post another person's camera footage, credentials, network details or device identifiers. Use synthetic examples and only investigate devices you own or have permission to access.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation when necessary. To report abuse, use GitHub's **Report content** tools; security defects belong in the private reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces. They do not create a promise of continuous moderation or support.
